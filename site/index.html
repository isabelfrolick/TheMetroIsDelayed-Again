        How this is measured

        Data comes from the STM's métro incident log on Montréal's open data portal. Each week a GitHub Action downloads it, adds new incidents to a stored history, recomputes these figures and republishes the page.

        A delay is a train incident that interrupted service for 5 minutes or more, timed from the start of the incident to the resumption of service. The STM leaves shorter interruptions out of its own reliability figures. An incident that affected several lines counts once for each line, and once in network totals. The chance of a delay is the share of past days on which a delay was under way at some point in that hour. Station rankings count delays recorded at a station between 5:00 and 1:00; incidents between stations (recorded as track sections) count toward their line but not toward any station. Yearly figures cover the months published so far.
